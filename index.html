TSGAM

TSGAM fits time series as a sum of components, including periodic patterns, trends, and responses to external variables such as temperature. The two notebooks below illustrate different questions you can ask of hourly load.

What explains hourly load?

Estimate held-out Los Angeles electricity demand from observed weather and calendar patterns. Inspect the fitted contributions and compare estimates with observations from the last week of each month.

What can I forecast from this hour?

Use a synthetic weather observation available at the forecast origin to predict demand at that hour and one, two, and three hours ahead.

To run either notebook locally, follow the example guide.
